# Brag Plan: Kpugi

## What is this app?
Kpugi is Nigeria's verified creator marketplace connecting advertisers with verified creators across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook, and LinkedIn—where creators get paid strictly per 1,000 verified impressions and brands buy confirmed organic reach backed by escrow.

## The angle
The era of fake screenshots, unverified view counts, and chasing influencer invoices is over. Kpugi brings institutional-grade proof-of-performance to Africa's creator economy: automated scrapers verify real human engagement across every major social network, releasing escrowed Naira straight to creator wallets within 72 hours.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold typography on obsidian dark glass with pulsing electric cobalt:
*"Where posts turn into payouts."*
A crisp simulated audio punch accompanied by the verified green badge settling into place.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Top Brands Live**: Real brand campaign badges appear sequentially (Piggyvest, Chowdeck, Infinix) showcasing live ₦ CPM rates.
- **The Audit Engine in Action**: A creator submission link is audited live across social networks; view counts tick up to 45,200 with 100% verification fidelity.
- **Instant Escrow Settlement**: Automatic budget release — ₦226,000 hits the creator wallet with green confetti and instant confirmation.

## Outro / punchline
The Kpugi logo reveals with verified trust marks:
*"Brands get real reach. Creators get paid."*
Call to Action: `kpugi.com — Launch Your Campaign`

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Brand publishes campaign with guaranteed CPM budget (e.g. PiggyVest, Chowdeck).
2. **Action**: Creator posts video and submits link -> Kpugi's multi-tier scraper engine audits authentic reach in real time.
3. **Result**: Automated escrow settlement releases payouts directly into the creator's wallet.
