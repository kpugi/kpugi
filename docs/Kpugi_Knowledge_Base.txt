KPUGI PLATFORM MASTER KNOWLEDGE BASE & OPERATIONS MANUAL

1. PLATFORM OVERVIEW
Kpugi is Nigeria's performance-based creator ad placement marketplace. We connect brands and advertisers with short-form content creators across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X (Twitter), Facebook, and LinkedIn. 

2. CREATOR EARNINGS & WEEKLY FRIDAY PAYOUTS
- Payout Schedule: Settlements are processed automatically every Friday afternoon.
- Payout Method: Direct bank transfer to any licensed Nigerian bank account (OPay, Kuda Bank, GTBank, Zenith Bank, Access Bank, First Bank, UBA, PalmPay, Moniepoint, etc.) via our payment gateway partner, Paystack.
- Creator Fees: Zero (₦0) withdrawal fees. Creators receive 90% of gross campaign earnings; Kpugi retains a 10% platform facilitation fee.
- Bank Account Setup: Creators add their 10-digit NUBAN account number and select their bank under "Wallet & Earnings" (/c/wallet). The account holder name is verified automatically via NIBSS/Paystack.
- Paystack Clarification: Paystack is our payment gateway/processor, not a bank. Creators do not need a Paystack account; funds go directly to their regular Nigerian bank account.

3. THE 1,000-VIEW MINIMUM THRESHOLD (HARD CLIFF)
- Follower Requirement: Zero (0) followers minimum. Any creator who can generate views can participate and earn.
- Milestone Requirement: A submitted post MUST hit at least 1,000 verified organic views before the campaign closes to unlock payout.
- Payout Formula: Gross Payout = (Verified Views / 1,000) * Campaign CPM Rate. Net Take-Home = Gross Payout * 0.90.
- Example: If a campaign pays ₦5,000 CPM, 10,000 views = ₦50,000 gross = ₦45,000 take-home pay.
- What if under 1,000 views: If a post finishes with under 1,000 views, the payout is ₦0. The reserved budget returns to the advertiser's active pool.

4. AUTOMATED VIEW AUDITING & QUALITY CONTROL
- Hourly Automated Audits: Once a creator submits a live post URL, our automated scraping and audit engine checks the post every 60 minutes.
- Supported Content Formats: TikTok videos, Instagram Reels & feed videos, YouTube Shorts, X (Twitter) posts and video posts, Facebook posts and reels.
- Public Requirement: Submitted posts must remain public and live. Archiving, privating, or deleting a post voids all pending earnings.
- Anti-Fraud & Authenticity Policy: Fake views, bot farms, loop auto-refreshers, or SMM click panels result in instant disqualification and permanent account termination.

5. BRAND CAMPAIGNS & 100% ESCROW PROTECTION
- Upfront Deposit: Advertisers fund 100% of their campaign budget upfront in Naira via Paystack into protected platform escrow before the campaign goes live.
- Pay-Per-View Guarantee: Advertisers only pay for verified, authentic views delivered by creators.
- Automatic Unspent Refunds: If a campaign closes without exhausting its full view goal, any unspent or unreserved budget is automatically refunded to the brand's wallet balance.
- Open Briefs: No manual creator approval bottleneck. Campaigns are open to all creators who meet the brief requirements.

6. CONNECTED ACCOUNTS & IDENTITY VERIFICATION (KYC)
- Social Account Verification (Code-in-Bio): We do NOT use OAuth or ask for social media logins. Creators connect accounts using our fast Bio Code verification:
  1. Enter your social handle on "Connected Accounts" (/c/accounts) for TikTok, Instagram, X (Twitter), or Facebook.
  2. Kpugi generates a unique temporary verification code (e.g. kpugi-a1b2c3d4e5).
  3. Go to your social profile → Edit Profile → paste the code anywhere into your bio → Save.
  4. Return to Kpugi and click "Verify Account". Our scraper checks your bio publicly and marks your account verified instantly. Once verified, you can remove the code from your bio.
- Identity KYC: Handled securely via Didit KYC. Acceptable IDs include National Identification Number (NIN), Nigerian International Passport, Driver's License, or INEC Voter's Card. KYC ensures the creator's legal name matches their bank account.

7. SUPPORT & HUMAN ESCALATION
- If a user has a specific account problem (missing Friday transfer, disputed audit view count, or KYC name change), KpugiBot collects their full name, email, and issue details and creates a support ticket for human staff triage.
