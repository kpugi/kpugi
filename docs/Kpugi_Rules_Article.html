Kpugi Creator Quality Standards & Compliance Rules

Document Identifier: KPUGI-DOC-CR-2026-V2.4 • Effective: September 1, 2026

Notice to All Creators: This document is the definitive regulatory compliance and operating framework for all creators on Kpugi. Participation in campaign drops, social verification, and Friday payouts are strictly governed by the standards detailed below.

1. The Operating Model & Independent Relationship

Kpugi is a performance-based ad placement marketplace operating on an automated Cost-Per-Mille (CPM) model. Compensation is calculated directly from verified, authentic organic video views delivered to brand campaign drops.

  • Open Briefs (Zero Gatekeeping): Any creator who has verified their identity and connected their channels can participate in open drops. Kpugi does not employ manual creator selection gatekeepers.
  • Independent Contractor Status: Creators operate strictly as independent contractors and are responsible for their own tax filings, business overheads, and local permits.

2. Creator Eligibility & Onboarding Standards

  • Age Requirement: Creators must be at least 18 years old. Minors aged 16–17 may participate solely with documented parental/guardian co-signing and legal representation.
  • One Human, One Account: You may register and operate only one (1) Kpugi profile. Operating duplicate, dummy, or leased accounts will trigger an immediate permanent ban.
  • Identity Verification (eKYC): Prior to receiving payouts, every creator must complete identity verification with a valid Nigerian government ID (NIN Slip / vNIN, International Passport, Driver's License, or INEC Voter's Card).
  • Bank Name Match Mandate: In strict compliance with CBN and NIBSS regulations, the legal name on your identity document must match precisely the account name on your Nigerian NUBAN bank account. Third-party account payouts are strictly rejected.
  • Code-in-Bio Social Verification: Kpugi never asks for your social media passwords or OAuth permissions. Channels are verified by temporarily placing a cryptographically random verification string (e.g. kpugi-v8f2a9x1) into your profile bio.

3. Campaign Participation & Brief Adherence

Each campaign drop outlines mandatory creative guidelines, visual hooks, talking points, and minimum video lengths. When claiming a drop:

  • Key Talking Points: Mandatory brand hooks and features must be shown clearly on screen for a minimum of three (3) uninterrupted seconds.
  • Competitor Exclusion: Do not feature or mention direct commercial competitors of the sponsoring brand within the same video.
  • Link-in-Bio & Prelanders: If a brief mandates a bio link, place the official Kpugi prelander link (kpugi.com/go/...) in your profile or pinned comment for the full campaign duration.

4. Mandatory Tagging, Ad Disclosures & Audio Licensing

  • Brand Mentions: Tag the exact sponsor account in the video caption using the exact spelling specified in the drop brief.
  • Campaign Hashtags: Include all required campaign hashtags (e.g., #KpugiDrop, #BrandCampaign) without typos.
  • ARCON & Consumer Ad Disclosures: Under Nigerian ARCON regulations and fair marketing standards, you must prominently include #ad, #sponsored, or #paidpartnership in the first 3 lines of your caption, and turn on the platform's native Branded Content toggle.
  • Commercial Audio Only: Always select music from commercial/royalty-free libraries. If your video is muted or removed for copyright infringement, all earnings on that video are voided (₦0.00).

5. The 1,000-View Minimum Threshold (The Hard Cliff)

The Hard Cliff Rule: A submitted video must reach at least 1,000 verified organic views before the campaign cutoff window closes to qualify for payout. Videos finishing with 999 views or fewer earn strictly ₦0.00.

6. 60-Minute Automated Audits & The 72-Hour Rule

  • Hourly Automated Scraping: Kpugi's audit engine scans your submitted video URL every 60 minutes to monitor view progress, tags, and availability.
  • The 72-Hour Public Retention Rule: Your video must remain continuously public and active for at least 72 hours (or the remaining campaign duration). Archiving, privating, or deleting the video forfeits all pending earnings and incurs a compliance strike.
  • No Post-Submission Edits: Removing tags or changing hashtags after submission will freeze your post for manual compliance review.

7. Zero Tolerance for Fraud & Fake Views

Absolute Prohibition: The use of SMM panels, bot farms, loop auto-refreshers, click rings, or engagement pods is strictly forbidden under platform policy and Nigerian cybercrime legislation.

Submissions detected with artificial traffic face immediate disciplinary action:

  1. Immediate drop disqualification with ₦0 payout.
  2. Complete forfeiture and clawback of unwithdrawn wallet balances.
  3. Permanent account revocation and institutional blacklisting of bank details and phone numbers.

8. Payouts, Escrow & Weekly Friday Settlements

  • 100% Escrow Protection: Advertisers fund campaign budgets into platform escrow upfront before drops go live, ensuring guaranteed payment upon audit completion.
  • Automatic Friday Settlements: Payout batches are disbursed automatically every Friday afternoon (12:00 PM – 6:00 PM WAT) directly into your verified Nigerian bank account via NIBSS/Paystack.
  • Zero (₦0) Withdrawal Fees: Kpugi charges zero withdrawal fees. Creators receive the full 90% net take-home pay.
  • Supported Institutions: All licensed Nigerian commercial banks (GTB, Zenith, Access, First Bank, UBA, etc.) and licensed fintechs (OPay, Kuda, PalmPay, Moniepoint, etc.).

9. The Three-Strike Compliance Matrix

Strike Level Infraction Trigger Platform Action
Strike 1 (Warning) Minor brief omission, audio muted post-publication. Drop rejected (₦0.00); formal written warning issued.
Strike 2 (Suspension) Early deletion within 72h, unauthorized caption alteration. Immediate disqualification; 14-day drop suspension.
Strike 3 (Terminal) Bot traffic, fake views, forged KYC, repeated Strike 2 infractions. Permanent account ban and financial blacklisting.

10. Audit Appeals & Human Support

If you believe an automated audit flagged your post due to third-party API latency or false positive tag detection, you may file an appeal within forty-eight (48) hours of campaign closure:

  • Submit an appeal directly via the KpugiBot AI Chat on your dashboard.
  • Or open an official support ticket at: support.kpugi.com/support/tickets/new
  • Provide your submission ID, video link, and an unedited screen recording of your native in-app video analytics. Decisions are rendered within two (2) business days.
