Kpugi Brand Operating Rules, Campaign Compliance & Escrow Standards

Document Identifier: KPUGI-DOC-BR-2026-V2.4 • Effective: September 1, 2026

The Kpugi Performance Guarantee: Advertisers on Kpugi only pay for real, human, verified organic views. 100% of campaign budgets are locked in automated escrow and released strictly as creator videos cross our 1,000-view audit threshold. This document is the definitive regulatory compliance and operating framework for all brands, businesses, and agencies advertising on Kpugi.

1. The Verified Performance Advertising Standard

Traditional influencer marketing in emerging markets forces brands to pay speculative upfront retainers with zero guarantee of audience delivery. Kpugi replaces speculative spend with an automated escrow and algorithmic view-auditing engine:

  • 100% Escrow Protection: Funds are locked securely in automated escrow before any creator can claim a slot or publish content.
  • The 1,000-View Hard Cliff: Creators earn ₦0.00 until their post crosses 1,000 verified organic views. If a video finishes under 1,000 views, the brand is charged ₦0.00, and the escrow funds remain the advertiser's property.
  • Automated 60-Minute Scraper Audits: Our server-side scrapers monitor live posts every 60 minutes across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and X (Twitter) for view velocity, retention, and authenticity.
  • Anti-Bot & Anti-Fraud Shield: Unnatural bot clusters, click farms, private videos, and deleted posts are automatically intercepted, scrubbed, and disqualified before touching brand capital.
  • The Brief as a Contract: The advertiser's campaign brief constitutes an enforceable Service Level Agreement (SLA). Creators must strictly comply with all mandatory guidelines to qualify for payout.

2. Advertiser Onboarding & Corporate KYB Verification

To safeguard platform integrity and protect consumers, Kpugi enforces corporate verification standards:

  • Corporate Eligibility: Registered companies (CAC in Nigeria, LLCs, PLCs), registered sole proprietorships, licensed advertising agencies, and verified independent eCommerce merchants are eligible to advertise.
  • Required Business Data: Advertisers must provide their legal trading name, corporate email address, Tax Identification Number (TIN) or CAC registration number, active website/social presence, and designated campaign manager contact info.
  • Dual-Role Separation: Creator Profiles and Advertiser Profiles are strictly partitioned. Users wishing to run campaigns and post content must maintain distinct operational profiles.
  • Authorized Billing Contacts: The registered business is solely liable for all campaign launches, budget approvals, and brief guidelines issued under its account credentials.

3. Campaign Economics, Budget Escrow & Fee Structure

Kpugi operates on a transparent Cost-Per-Mille (CPM) pricing model backed by 100% pre-funded escrow:

  • Cost-Per-Mille (CPM): Cost per 1,000 verified views. Minimum CPM floor is ₦2,000 per 1k views (₦2.00 per verified view). Recommended presets: ₦2,000 | ₦2,500 | ₦3,500 | ₦5,000 | ₦7,500 | ₦10,000+. Higher CPMs attract faster participation from premium creators.
  • Campaign Minimums: Starter budget minimum is ₦10,000 (recommended standard: ₦25,000 to ₦100,000; Enterprise scale: ₦500,000 to ₦10,000,000+).
  • Hard Budget Cap: Campaigns automatically pause the moment verified creator views reach the budget cap. You will never be billed for runaway views.
  • Featured Campaign Boost: Brands may select a "Featured Campaign" add-on (₦2,500 flat fee) for sticky top-of-browse placement and high-priority push notifications to verified creators.
THE 90 / 10 REVENUE ALLOCATION MODEL:
• 90% Creator Earnings Pool — Locked in escrow, disbursed strictly per 1,000 verified views.
• 10% Kpugi Platform Fee — Covers automated 60-min scrapers, bot detection, eKYC, and Paystack banking rails.

Example: A ₦100,000 campaign allocates ₦90,000 directly to creators delivering verified views, and ₦10,000 to platform infrastructure.

Unspent Escrow Balances & Refunds:

  • Unspent escrow funds remain 100% the property of the advertiser.
  • If a campaign ends or is archived before the full budget is exhausted, remaining funds are immediately unlocked and credited to your Brand Wallet.
  • Cash refunds from your Brand Wallet back to your registered Nigerian corporate bank account are processed via Paystack within 3 to 5 business days.

4. Campaign Brief Construction & Enforceability

Your campaign brief is a legally binding specification. When creating your brief:

  • Mandatory Brief Fields: Must include a clear Campaign Title, Campaign Objective (App Installs, Awareness, Product Drop), Description, Mandatory Talking Points (2 to 4 key propositions), Specific Do's and Don'ts, and Required Tags.
  • Required Tags & Disclosures: Briefs must require creators to include #KpugiAd, #Sponsored, or #Ad, plus the brand's official handle (e.g. @YourBrandName).
  • Asset Provision: Brands should provide cloud links (Google Drive, Dropbox) to high-resolution logos, audio/voiceover files, and visual guidelines.
  • The Freeze Rule (No Retroactive Alterations): Once a creator claims a slot and submits a live video, the brand cannot retroactively introduce new requirements or modify mandatory hashtags for that submission. Changes only apply to subsequent slot claims.

6. Advertising Law, ARCON Compliance & Consumer Protection

  • Advertiser Legal Liability: Under the Advertising Regulatory Council of Nigeria (ARCON) Act, the advertiser holds sole legal responsibility for all performance claims, discounts, health benefits, and product guarantees stated in campaign briefs.
  • Mandatory Disclosure Rule: Advertisers must instruct creators to clearly disclose commercial sponsorship using #Ad, #Sponsored, or #PaidPartnership in post captions. Disguising ads as organic personal endorsements violates FCCPC consumer protection laws.
  • Truth in Advertising: Misleading pricing, deceptive subscription traps, or non-existent promotional prizes are strictly prohibited.

7. Intellectual Property & Content Licensing

  • Brand Asset Ownership: The advertiser warrants that it owns or has authorized commercial licenses for all logos, music, trademarks, and scripts provided in the campaign brief, indemnifying Kpugi and participating creators.
  • Organic Distribution License: Payout covers public organic hosting on the creator's channel for a minimum of 72 consecutive hours (and perpetually thereafter, subject to standard creator profile management).
  • Reposting Rights: The brand is granted a non-exclusive license to repost, share, or embed the live creator video on official brand social channels with creator attribution.
  • Paid Media Whitelisting (Spark Ads / Meta Partnership Ads): The standard CPM payout covers organic reach only. Brands wishing to run creator posts as dark ads or boosted paid creatives must obtain creator authorization or negotiate whitelisting codes through Kpugi support.
  • Case Studies: Kpugi reserves the right to showcase public video links, engagement metrics, and brand logos in anonymized marketing case studies.

8. View Auditing, Fraud Protection & Brand Safeguards

Kpugi's verification engine protects brand capital through automated, algorithmic checks:

  • 60-Minute Scraper Audits: Automated server-side polling queries public endpoints every 60 minutes, recording verified view counts, velocity curves, and interaction ratios.
  • Bot Traffic Filtering: Traffic originating from click farms, datacenter proxy networks, or automated view-bot clusters is identified and excluded from payable view counts.
  • 72-Hour Public Retention Enforcement: If a creator deletes, archives, or sets a video to "Private" before 72 hours have elapsed, the submission is disqualified, pending earnings are canceled, and reserved escrow funds are refunded to the advertiser's Brand Wallet.

9. Creator Review, Brief Disputes & 48-Hour Arbitration

Advertisers can dispute submissions that fail to comply with explicit brief instructions:

  • Valid Dispute Grounds: Missing mandatory tags (#KpugiAd, #Ad), missing brand handle tag (@BrandName), promoting direct competitors, violating explicit Do's and Don'ts, audio removal/muting, or deleting the video before 72 hours.
  • Invalid Dispute Grounds: Subjective artistic dissatisfaction (facial expressions, acting style, voice tone) where all explicit brief guidelines were met; or demanding requirements not in the original brief.
  • 48-Hour Arbitration SLA: File a dispute via your dashboard or support within 48 hours of submission. Kpugi's compliance team will review the video against the brief and render a binding decision. Upheld disputes cancel creator payouts and restore escrow funds to your Brand Wallet.

11. Brand Code of Conduct & Penalty Matrix

  • Anti-Circumvention (Non-Poaching): Brands are strictly prohibited from using Kpugi as a talent directory to solicit creators off-platform to circumvent escrow and platform fees.
  • Professional Communication: All interactions in campaign briefs, support tickets, and dispute channels must remain respectful and free from harassment or abusive remarks.
  • Violation Penalties: Minor infractions incur brief revisions; repeated bad-faith disputes result in dispute suspension; off-platform poaching or promoting prohibited goods leads to immediate campaign suspension, escrow forfeiture, and permanent business blacklisting.

Pre-Flight Campaign Launch Checklist

Before publishing your campaign, ensure you have satisfied the following standards:

  • Product does not appear on the Prohibited Categories list.
  • Brief specifies 2 to 4 mandatory value propositions or talking points.
  • Explicit Do's and Don'ts regarding competitor mentions and audio usage are stated.
  • Mandatory tags (#KpugiAd, #Sponsored, @YourBrandName) are required in caption.
  • High-resolution assets, audio files, or logos are shared via open cloud links.
  • Budget and CPM rate meet minimum floors (min ₦2,000 CPM; min ₦10,000 budget).
  • Escrow funds are deposited, ensuring guaranteed creator payout upon reaching 1,000+ verified views.
